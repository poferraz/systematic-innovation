# Innovation Lenses, Reasoning Cells & Mechanism Ledger

The named Reasoning Cells are a **Project-original AI-native mechanism**. Their internal lenses draw on verified principles and adapted creative-problem-solving methods; neither the cell taxonomy nor its labels should be attributed to Tudor Rickards.

## 1. Two-Stage Anti-Typicality Protocol (Countering Modal Collapse)

Rickards's work on initial fluency depletion (1975; 1985, Ch. 3 pp. 55–57) and recent post-training typicality research motivate clearing familiar associations before structural generation. The quarantine procedure below is an AI-native adaptation, not a universal empirical claim.

Before populating the Mechanism Ledger:
1. **Negative-Space Trope Quarantine (1–2 lines):** Explicitly name the 2–3 highest-probability textbook or internet tropes for the request (e.g., *"Generic app reminders / gamified streaks / fee penalties"*). Exclude any draft whose causal dynamic or verb-object structure repeats a quarantined trope.
2. **Tail-Distribution Exploration:** Draw candidate mechanisms across genuinely different structural coordinates in the conditional space (incentive, timing, custody, physical flow, subtraction) instead of near-mode paraphrases. Verbalize diversity dimensions cleanly in ledger coordinates without inflating prose.

## 2. The Seven Reasoning Cells

Quick Mode uses **System Dynamics plus two selected Reasoning Cells**. Deep Mode uses **five or six selected Reasoning Cells**. Each selected cell produces **Two to four candidate mechanisms**, their intervention point, a concise causal explanation, and a distinction from mechanisms already present.

### Cross-Cell Combinatorial & Reframing Operators
- **Morphological recombination (2x2 forcing):** Decompose the tension along two or more independent causal axes (for example, *when intervention happens* by *who holds incentive or artifact*) in a compact 2x2 grid and fill sparse non-default cells.
- **Liability-to-Asset Reversal:** Test flipping a named friction, delay, queue, or waste stream into an informative signal, coordination trigger, or protective buffer inside safe boundaries.
- **Subtraction:** Remove a legacy step, status artifact, or intermediate handoff rule to test whether value improves without violating core invariants.

### Cell Definitions
- **System Dynamics:** Change feedback loops, accumulations, delays, or high-impact control points where small rule shifts alter equilibrium behavior.
- **Human Value:** Reconfigure dignity, trust, cognitive load, accessibility, or participation by surfacing anxiety, status loss, or hidden work.
- **Operational Reality:** Remove bottlenecks, rework, and repeated handling by reusing existing touchpoints, idle windows, or physical geometry without adding unstated staff, funding, or permissions.
- **Boundary Reframing:** Shift objective, scope, ownership, or decision rights. Run multiple redefinitions—analytic, empathic, motivational, metaphorical, and boundary-relaxed—before locking directions.
- **Mechanism Transfer (Metaphor-Stripped):** Import from a structurally relevant outside domain by extracting a **two-node abstract causal or dependency structure** (actors, flow, bottleneck, feedback) first, then stripping all source-domain jargon before phrasing the candidate. Domain labels without structural isomorphism are invalid transfers.
- **Contrarian:** Reverse one named assumption and trace consequences; translate every raw provocation into a constraint-compliant, safe operational mechanism.
- **Futures:** Test resilience and unlock new designs by shifting time horizon, scale, scarcity, technology, or regulatory conditions.

## 3. Parallel Execution & Sequential Fallback

When the host provides independent sub-contexts, selected Reasoning Cells may run in parallel and return mechanisms with explicit structural coordinates. Otherwise, execute sequential contrastive passes, delay synthesis until passes complete, and never claim sequential passes were independent agents.

## 4. Mechanism Ledger, Duplicate Test & Clustered Variety Floor

Track each candidate's causal coordinates inline or compactly: mechanism family, intervention point, principal actor, changed flow, intended effect, variants, and originating cell/lens. Compare structural operations (timing, scale, decision rights, incentives, value delivery, feedback):

1. **Compare causal operations, not surface labels:** Merge wording or channel variants carrying the exact same underlying causal mechanism. Keep mechanisms that differ causally or across at least two structural dimensions even if they share generic labels.
2. **Clustered Variety Floor:** For standard non-budget-limited runs, avoid premature stopping before exploring **at least five distinct causal clusters** across at least two Reasoning Cells (unless true finite-space domain saturation is demonstrated and explained). Keep promising rough candidates separated across distinct clusters until duplicate checks verify structural equivalence. Under hard word ceilings, budget precedence overrides this floor.
3. **Minimum Quality Gate:** Every candidate must be relevant, constraint-aligned, causally coherent, plausible, and safe.

## 5. Currie `5W+Why` Invariant Audit & Adversarial Feasibility

Before finalizing any candidate, audit resources, authority, geometry, capacity, and preservation:
- **Separate Invariant from Legacy Delivery Vehicle:** Using Currie-style questioning (*what* core protection, outcome, or stakeholder value must stay intact vs. *which* **legacy delivery vehicle** currently delivers it), verify candidates protect the invariant without locking in broken routines or unconsenting workload shifts.
- **Zero Smuggled Inventory:** Distinguish supplied baseline resources from newly demanded equipment, budget, consents, or permissions. Label unverified parameters rather than asserting feasibility.
- **Cumulative Re-Check:** On every new multi-turn disclosure, re-verify every candidate's actual causal chain and resource demand against the full `Preserve:` ledger. Regulatory evasion and safety concealment remain excluded.
