# Analysis contract (draft)

This study estimates systematic ideation performance. It does not measure realized innovation, global novelty, or market success. The primary contrast is full skill minus format-matched control on qualified useful distinct mechanisms per 1,000 total assistant output tokens. Other main conditions are confirmatory secondary contrasts; ablations are development-only and follow evidence of a full-treatment effect.

Extract candidates only from the final portfolio. Preserve every extracted candidate and raw causal family. Apply all five gates conjunctively to each candidate before deduplicating qualifying candidates: relevance, constraint alignment, and causal clarity each at least 2/3; plausibility at least 1/3; no critical safety or rights violation. Use structural and equivalence evidence for families and one-to-one maximum-weight Hungarian assignment for reference recovery. Actor substitutions alone do not establish a new family. Preserve judge evidence and disputed equivalence decisions.

For a completed generation use `1000 * qualified_distinct_families / total_assistant_output_tokens`. Every assistant turn, including questioning and retries with observed usage, contributes to resource disclosures. Never replace missing total tokens with final-response tokens or an estimate presented as provider metadata. Retain transport failures after bounded retries, refusals, timeouts, empty or malformed responses, and their observed resources as outcomes, with zero qualified mechanisms. Missing or corrupt total tokens makes the primary efficiency score missing, not zero; disclose the resulting primary-eligibility count separately from all attempted outcomes.

Average repetitions within case, condition, model, and host first, then form paired case differences under the same configuration. Resample whole cases with replacement, preserving all arms and repetitions of a case together. Report mean and median paired effects, 95% percentile and BCa intervals, sample size, missing pairs, and win/tie/loss using the frozen equivalence margin. Do not treat repetitions as independent cases. Use 10,000 seeded resamples; the public analysis seed controls numerical reproducibility only and never human blinding. BCa degeneracy and small-sample limitations must remain visible rather than yielding a spurious interval. Adjust the family of secondary-contrast p values with Holm's step-down procedure. Report domain/model crossed factors only when supported by sample size.

Disclose extracted candidates, raw distinct families, qualified candidates, qualified distinct families, rejections separately for every gate criterion, final-response tokens, and all-assistant tokens beside the primary result. Report confirmatory outcomes, efficiency, failure/refusal rates, latency, available cost, calibration, and raw diversity. Separate single-turn and controlled multi-turn estimates before the preregistered aggregate. Family and domain heterogeneity are exploratory unless separately registered. Judge abstentions/failures are missing with counts and pessimistic sensitivity bounds; provide complete-case and failure-inclusive sensitivity summaries without silently dropping difficult cases. Report negative, null, and inconsistent order results.

Pilot derivations are declared in `preregistration.yaml`: practical effect is 0.20 times the difference between Q75 and Q25 of format-matched case primary scores; equivalence is 0.10 times that difference. Zero or unavailable dispersion blocks freeze and requires a new approved derivation, never an invented outcome. Choose the smallest repetition count giving median case endpoint standard error at most 20% of the practical effect, subject to the authorized budget. Freeze numeric variance, reliability, margins, repetitions, measurement versions, candidate/runner revisions, and digests before validation. A frozen declaration with missing numeric values is invalid.

Validation yields only the preregistered release-eligibility decision to candidate development. Its case-level effects, directionality, strata, annotations, failure examples, and hidden labels cannot guide revisions. A failure rejects that candidate; later revisions need independent public development justification and a new candidate version. The sealed test executes once after freeze and unchanged candidate verification, then proceeds to reporting and stop. Before 10% of parent jobs complete, an infrastructure-only failure may restart under a new run ID only without inspecting effects; later interruption resumes the same run's next pending turn. No sealed outcome authorizes tuning. Quarantine contamination, formally retire affected cases, close the study, replace evidence, and record the transition before any future development use.

Automated headline claims require accepted human calibration. Otherwise humans score the primary endpoint and LLM judgments remain exploratory. An optional composite needs preregistered weights and sensitivity analysis and never replaces the primary endpoint. Broad cross-agent claims require separate behavioral evidence from at least three families and independent compatibility dimensions; they are not inferred from the controlled primary experiment.
