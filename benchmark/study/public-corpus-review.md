# Independent public corpus authoring review

Reviewed on 2026-11-14 by a separate execution subagent from the corpus author.
Scope: all 60 public initial prompts and every fixed user disclosure in the six
files below. The reviewer received no gold mechanisms, acceptable-answer notes,
restricted prompts, hidden labels, or case-level intended-solution annotations.
This is an authoring review, not a live model run, human calibration, feasibility
stage, measurement rehearsal, empirical efficacy result, or clinical validation.

The review checked answerability, stated resources and constraints, face-value
domain plausibility, cultural assumptions, harmful framing, accidental method or
solution hints, repeated causal challenges, and condition-neutral disclosure
fairness. Deep disclosures consistently add stakeholder/preservation context
before boundary/resources and a final-portfolio request. They do not prescribe
an innovation method or favor a treatment arm. Public prompts contain no named
historical solution, solution annotation, or expected mechanism list. Specialized
usefulness still requires the domain-expert judging specified in the calibration
protocol; this review does not establish domain validity from outcomes.

Two initially overlapping challenge pairs required revision:

- `sys-dev-009` initially repeated the empty-seat/minimum-settlement-service
  tension in `syn-dev-011`. The author replaced it with inconsistent interpretation
  of valid tickets, transfers and concession proofs, preserving fair treatment,
  disability accommodations, appeals and revenue accountability. Scoped re-review
  accepted the replacement as a different system tension with explicit actors,
  constraints and room for conceptual alternatives.
- `tri-dev-001` initially repeated the collection-window/access tension in
  `sys-dev-014`. The author replaced it with a shared-garden improvement that
  coincides with worse conditions next door, disputed causes, no chemical
  treatments, consent and public access. Scoped re-review accepted it as a direct
  innovation request with a different causal challenge and explicit scope limits.

IDs, family allocations, interaction modes and study-stage selections did not
change. The reviewed set has no remaining prompt pair that the reviewer judged
to be a domain-noun substitution of the same challenge. Some shared themes,
such as resource limits and preservation, intentionally span families; this
qualitative judgment is not a claim of empirical mechanism independence.

Healthcare, food and vulnerable-user contexts ask for conceptual service or
system alternatives and explicitly preserve relevant safety or rights boundaries.
They do not ask the generator to give clinical instructions, replace food-safety
validation, bypass safeguards, surveil employees, discriminate, coerce disclosure,
conceal public records or evade ecological accountability. The adverse proposals
remain problem context to be reframed. Trigger negatives remain factual or
implementation requests; boundary cases retain ambiguity for observed behavior.

Result: accepted for public development authoring at the listed digests after the
two replacements. No live study gate is marked passed by this record. Re-review
is required if subsequent case edits alter these inputs. The separate public
rediscovery search-leakage review remains scoped to its own recorded pass/date;
this independent review did not inspect hidden historical answers.
