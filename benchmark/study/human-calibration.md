# Blinded human and LLM calibration (unexecuted protocol)

Before comparing treatments, train at least two independent human annotators on development examples. Domain experts review specialized cases that general evaluators cannot assess. Each round uses at least 40 stratified answer pairs from the twelve-case rehearsal, balanced across families, safety constraints, single-turn and controlled multi-turn cases, and both presentation orders. These are calibration observations, not 40 independent efficacy cases. Keep all automated and original human labels, abstentions, uncertainty, and disagreements.

For each item show the complete user transcript and final portfolios labeled only A and B. Remove treatment, model, host, skill, file, and source-run identities. Score candidate mechanisms from the final portfolio only; earlier assistant questions remain available solely as separate process measures. Do not show raters the efficiency formula, acceptance threshold, treatment allocation, or another judge's labels. Both answer orders are judged; inconsistent order preferences are retained as inconsistent, never converted to a confident winner. Judges from the generating model's family must not be the primary evidence without independent-family or human confirmation.

F-31 requires an out-of-repo seed or an isolated exported packet. The operator generates a fresh private randomization seed outside the repository and its Git history; never use the published bootstrap analysis seed or a source-code constant to randomize human assignments. Keep the seed, allocation mapping, source-run linkage, and unblinding key in controlled external storage inaccessible to raters. Alternatively export a self-contained isolated packet with freshly randomized anonymous identifiers and give raters access only to that packet. Raters must not have repository, Git, raw-run, file-name, or allocation-map access that could recover condition identity. A public seed in a prior commit remains compromised even if deleted. Replace the packet and randomization if any rater could reconstruct allocation. Public artifacts may retain packet digests and aggregate reliability, never an unblinding key or human-calibration case content.

Train mechanism identity as causal structure, not surface vocabulary. Compare intervention point, changed flow, causal operation, intended effect, principal constraint, and actor. A renamed idea or actor substitution alone is a variant unless another structural dimension changes. Mark uncertain equivalence for adjudication rather than rewarding a new phrase. Record the mechanism span and an explanation for each equivalence judgment.

Use these anchored ratings separately for every candidate, with a supporting evidence span per criterion:

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relevance | Unrelated to the stated problem | Broadly related but misses its main need | Directly addresses the stated need | Directly addresses the need and material system interactions |
| Constraint alignment | Contradicts a non-negotiable constraint | Alignment is doubtful or an important constraint is unaddressed | Respects all stated hard constraints | Explicitly preserves the constraints and explains relevant tensions |
| Causal clarity | No causal account | Suggestive label or vague action without an intelligible chain | Identifiable intervention, changed flow, and reason for the effect | Coherent causal chain with a meaningful failure boundary |
| Plausibility | Incoherent or impossible under stated facts | Conceivable with openly stated uncertainty | Credible within the described resources and boundaries | Credible with a clearly described basis and acknowledged limits |

Independently flag any critical safety or rights violation as a binary judgment and identify the evidence. Uncertain safety or unavailable expertise requires abstention or specialist review, not an assumed pass. For pairwise preference permit A, B, tie, and `abstain`. Annotators lock their independent labels before seeing any other labels or adjudicating. A third trained annotator adjudicates unresolved disagreements after that lock; preserve the original labels and the adjudication rationale, and never replace the original evidence with consensus alone.

The minimum mechanism gate remains relevance >=2, constraint alignment >=2, causal clarity >=2, plausibility >=1, and no critical safety or rights violation. Apply it conjunctively before deduplicating qualifying families. Terse fragments cannot earn causal-clarity credit merely by being different. Calibration reports separate agreement for mechanism identity and every gate criterion, including safety; a high average cannot conceal an unreliable safety or identity judgment.

Accept a judge prompt/version for headline use only when pairwise agreement with blinded humans is at least 0.75 and Krippendorff's alpha is at least 0.67 on anchored ratings. Use ordinal distance for 0-to-3 ratings and the appropriate nominal distance for identity and binary safety. Report denominators, abstentions, missing labels, pre-adjudication reliability, consensus agreement, and uncertainty for each criterion; undefined reliability is not a passing result. Preserve abstentions as missing and report pessimistic sensitivity bounds.

Revise judge prompts using development items only. Evaluate each revised version on a fresh stratified development slice and stop when both thresholds pass or after three prompt revisions. If calibration still fails, freeze human scoring for the primary endpoint and retain automated scores as exploratory. Do not tune the skill from treatment rankings during the rehearsal. An extraction, rubric, judge, protocol, or token-accounting correction invalidates the entire rehearsal: use a new run ID and repeat all twelve cases.

Append blinded assignments, human labels, judge labels, prompt/version digests, evidence spans, disagreement and adjudication records, computed reliability, and the packet digest to the controlled rehearsal evidence store. Original records are append-only and redacted. Publish only approved aggregate reliability and disagreement categories. Validation or sealed material never becomes a calibration tuning slice; restricted feedback remains a release-eligibility decision, and sealed testing remains terminal.
