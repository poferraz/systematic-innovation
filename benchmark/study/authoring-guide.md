# Public case authoring

Write one answerable problem per case in natural language. Include observable actors, a system tension, and constraints a reviewer can check. Leave room for several causally different possibilities. Vary where the problem sits in a system rather than substituting industry nouns in a repeated template. Do not imply that novelty is always beneficial; maintaining, simplifying, or stopping can be useful possibilities.

The manifest fixes family allocations and strata. Public case files contain only development prompts, strata, interaction metadata, expected mode, and trigger expectations. They contain no gold mechanisms, answer keys, solution names, source URLs, distractors, private notes, rubrics, or judge instructions. Rediscovery cases abstract a pre-solution problem state without names, dates, distinctive quotations, patents, or solution hints; their labels are contamination-prone by design and cannot establish global novelty.

Deep cases use exactly two fixed disclosures. The first adds stakeholder and preservation information; the second adds boundary and resource information and ends with a request for the final portfolio. The controller supplies these byte-identically to all conditions, regardless of questions asked. Disclosures cannot name an ideation method or reveal expected mechanisms. Quick and trigger-negative cases have no disclosure script.

Before a study uses a corpus version, an independent mechanism-blind reviewer checks answerability, domain plausibility, cultural assumptions, harmful framing, accidental solution hints, near-duplicates, fair disclosures, and contamination by skill terminology. Predict likely intervention families from prompts without seeing private labels; rewrite cases where wording directly supplies multiple intended families or where over half the predicted families duplicate another case. Case-level semantic review is a separate study gate; schema and text-uniqueness checks do not substitute for it.

For each rediscovery prompt, search one distinctive sentence and rewrite if its known solution is directly identified on the first result page. The public review record retains only case ID, pass/fail, and review date. Do not publish search terms paired with hidden answers or private companion notes.

Generation receives no rubric, hidden mechanism, judge note, treatment label, or other condition's output. Private scoring companions require protected provenance, acceptable functional equivalents, unsafe-output definitions, deterministic checks, and two independent reviewer approvals. Keep those records outside the worktree and automatic agent discovery. A restricted case influencing candidate development is retired and replaced before reuse; the transition requires a study decision record.
