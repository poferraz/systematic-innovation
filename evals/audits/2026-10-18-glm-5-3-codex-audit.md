# Independent Audit Report: systematic-innovation v2 Evolution Plan

## Executive Audit Summary

The proposed v2 evolution is directionally strong because it preserves the skill’s four core guardrails: ideation-first, preservation-first, causal distinction, and provider-neutral portability. The plain-English boundary probes, four systemic-failure archetypes, and functional-invariant/structural-vehicle distinction are high-leverage additions for real organizational and engineering systems. They are also compatible with the existing Quick/Deep architecture and do not require a new public methodology, a new reasoning cell, or a host-specific adapter.

The main weakness is not conceptual emptiness but implementation ambiguity. The v2 synthesis is right to reject CESM and MLP as formal, token-heavy frameworks, but the triage is too binary: useful parts of those theories—structural bonds, multi-scale lock-in, transition buffers, and environmental conditions—should still survive as compact internal dimensions rather than disappear. The root-file additions are small enough to remain token-safe, yet they may be too easy for smaller or mid-tier models to ignore unless they are imperative and testable. The “functional invariant vs. structural vehicle” distinction is valuable but exploitable unless compliance, safety, rights, privacy, and physical limits are hard-coded as invariants rather than negotiable implementation choices. The proposed benchmark additions are promising but not yet falsifiable enough to support a headline effect claim without precise definitions, controlled comparisons, and human calibration.

The architecture should not yet modify canonical files. It should first convert the v2 ideas into a few explicit behavioral contracts and test those contracts against realistic Quick, Deep, safety, portability, and efficiency cases. Overall verdict: **REVISE**.

## Pass 1: Theoretical Systems Scholar (Fidelity & Omission)
- **Findings on Triage (CESM, MLP, TIS, CSH):** The triage is mostly sound but incomplete. Rejecting CESM and MLP as explicit public frameworks is defensible: their formal notation and macro-transition terminology would inflate tokens and invite performative scholarship. However, wholesale rejection risks discarding two causal signals that the current architecture handles only implicitly. CESM’s useful residue is that outcomes emerge from **composition, environment, structure, and mechanism**, so a candidate is not just “who does what,” but also which contracts, power relations, dependencies, and external rules make the behavior possible. MLP’s useful residue is that **scale and transition buffers** matter: an apparently local intervention can be blocked by a regime-level standard, sunk infrastructure, or missing market. The current Futures and Boundary Reframing cells can absorb those signals if the v2 contract tells the model to scan them explicitly. Otherwise, the rejection leaves an explanatory hole in exactly the cases the v2 additions are meant to improve.
- **Boundary Critique & Plain-English Translation:** The three natural probes—externalized burden, incommensurable scorecards, and untouchable anchors—capture the causal core of Ulrich and Midgley without academic ceremony. They expose affected parties, decision rights, missing evidence, and boundary judgments. The emancipatory core survives if the skill asks *who bears costs, who decides, and whose evidence is missing* in neutral, operational language. It would be diluted if the model only treated affected parties as data or as a stakeholder-inventory formality. The correct stance is not moral advocacy; it is explicit mapping of externalities, voice, and authority.
- **Systemic Failure Theoretical Rigor:** The four adapted systemic-failure archetypes are defensible as operational heuristics. They are not a new theory of innovation; they are compact causal patterns for identifying why otherwise plausible interventions stall. Their value depends on keeping them tied to structural coordinates rather than letting them become a quota or decorative taxonomy. The archetypes also overlap, especially when a missing complement is also a network and legitimation problem. That overlap is acceptable in a generative sweep but must not become a forced classification exercise.
- **Pass 1 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **REVISE.** Preserve the public rejection of formal CESM/MLP language, but add a compact internal rule: the system scan must consider **actor composition, environment, structural bonds, mechanism, scale, and transition buffer**. The three boundary probes should remain user-facing, but the model must internally connect them to decision rights and affected parties, not merely to “stakeholders.” The four failure modes should be used as a mandatory internal sweep, not as a separate public methodology.

## Pass 2: Agent Capability Architect (Token Economy & Context Budget)
- **Prompt Economy Impact on SKILL.md:** The proposal is token-safe at the proposed size. The canonical root is already compact and well-structured, and an addition of a few lines can be justified if it changes behavior rather than adds academic text. Enriching Brief Fields 2 and 8 is preferable to adding new fields because it reuses existing slots and does not fragment the model’s task graph. However, the additions should be **prompts, not extra inventories**; they should say who absorbs downstream burden and which constraints are treated as fixed, not require a separate visible list.
- **Instruction-Following Risk (70B/8B Tier):** The main risk is salience, not raw token count. Smaller and mid-tier models tend to follow short, imperative instructions that are near the point of use and are easy to check. If the new requirements are only buried in long reference prose, they may be skipped. The v2 design should therefore put one concise rule in the root and one explicit coverage check near the ledger or system scan. Avoid nested academic definitions, synonym chains, or separate moral framing.
- **Reasoning Cell Taxonomy Evaluation:** Keeping the four failure modes as internal generative heuristics is better than adding an eighth Reasoning Cell. A new cell would create a quota and crowd out the current seven transformations, especially in Quick Mode. But “internal only” is not enough. The model needs a **mandatory Systemic Failure Sweep** before finalization: check each archetype, record which one is relevant, and either generate from it or explain why it is inapplicable. That preserves breadth without adding a new cell.
- **Pass 2 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **REVISE.** Keep the root addition under the proposed line budget, but make the new requirements imperative and observable. Specifically: keep Fields 2 and 8, add a one-line failure-mode sweep, and require a ledger check that at least one relevant systemic failure pattern was considered. Put explanatory detail in references, not the root. Do not add an eighth cell.

## Pass 3: Enterprise Engineering Practitioner (User Friction & Demeanor)
- **Real-World Engineering Utility:** The additions map well to recurring enterprise deadlocks: CI/CD bottlenecks caused by ownership boundaries, distributed-systems latency caused by hidden handoffs, legacy migrations trapped by procedural habits, vendor lock-in, and cross-team scorecards that reward different behaviors. Asking who bears the downstream cost, whose success definition conflicts, and which rule is treated as untouchable is often more useful than asking for more technical detail alone. These questions surface the real blocking mechanism, not just the visible symptom.
- **Discovery Question Friction in Deep Mode:** The three probes are high-leverage but need to remain natural. “Who ends up absorbing the downstream pain when this workflow breaks?” is natural. “Which groups are using different scorecards to judge the same result?” is natural. “Which rules or habits are currently treated as fixed, and who could change them?” is natural. Terms like “incommensurable scorecards” and “untouchable anchors” should remain internal labels, not necessarily user-facing vocabulary. Deep Mode should select one prompt per turn based on the observed tension, not ask all three every time.
- **Neutrality & Demeanor Verification:** The plan’s systems-engineer tone is the right one. Boundary critique should be expressed through externalities, decision rights, evidence gaps, and cost transfer rather than moral or political judgment. The skill should name structural asymmetries without assigning blame or prescribing an ideological remedy. That is compatible with the current guardrail that stakeholder reactions remain hypotheses rather than invented research.
- **Pass 3 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **REVISE.** Keep the three probes, but write user-facing versions in plain operational language and choose only the most relevant one for the current tension. Preserve neutrality by asking about observable costs, authority, and missing evidence. Do not turn the questions into a mandatory interrogation.

## Pass 4: Adversarial Safety & Preservation Auditor (Constraint Integrity)
- **Stress-Testing "Functional Invariant vs. Structural Vehicle":** The distinction is useful and necessary. It prevents the preservation rule from protecting legacy habits simply because they are old. A vehicle is a **replaceable implementation mechanism**—manual sign-off, ticket ritual, report format, routing rule, or tooling sequence. An invariant is a **required outcome or floor**—safety, privacy, medical safety, rights, contract validity, legal compliance, physical limits, or core functioning value. The v2 plan must make that precedence explicit.
- **Exploit & Bypass Potential:** Without a hard precedence rule, an adversarial prompt could label privacy, compliance, retention, or safety review as a “structural vehicle” and propose replacing or weakening it. The skill should therefore state: statutory, safety, privacy, rights, medical, contractual, and physical constraints are **invariants unless the user explicitly changes the target and the alternative remains compliant**. If the user asks to weaken one, the model must surface the contradiction rather than silently reclassify it. This should be a blocking rule, not a preference.
- **"Yes-But" Inoculation Robustness:** Rickards’s *Yes-but* reflex is valuable, but it should not produce defensive boilerplate. A useful spotlight should name the most plausible gatekeeper objection and show the safeguard that preserves the core mechanism. It should stay concise and evidence-limited. Generic reassurance such as “this may face resistance but can be managed” would be weaker than naming a specific authority, resource, or compliance condition.
- **Pass 4 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **REVISE.** Add a three-way constraint classification before generation: **invariant**, **vehicle**, or **unknown**. Any unknown safety or rights issue must be surfaced as a risk, not silently relaxed. Vehicles may change only if the invariant outcome is preserved at the same or stronger level. The *Yes-but* safeguard should be concise and tied to a specific objection.

## Pass 5: Empirical Benchmark & Measurement Auditor (Falsifiability)
- **Metric Objectivity & Measurement Rigor:** Vendi Score is mathematically objective once a kernel is declared, so it is suitable as a judge-free diversity diagnostic. But the current implementation uses lexical and structured-field similarity, which means it can still miss semantic equivalence or split the same operation because of different wording. Process Tweak Depletion Rate and Failure-Mode Span are promising but need exact operational definitions before they can be benchmark endpoints. Invariant Preservation is measurable only when the invariants are explicit and the scoring rule distinguishes genuine preservation from a repeated “preserve check” sentence.
- **Automated Reproducibility vs. Subjective Bias:** The current benchmark design correctly separates deterministic extraction, structural clustering, blind judging, and human calibration. That separation should be preserved. A regex or keyword-only scorer is not enough for causal equivalence; a pure LLM-as-judge approach is not enough for headline claims. The best path is deterministic field extraction plus blinded multi-judge evaluation with criterion-specific evidence and calibrated human agreement.
- **Expected Token-Normalized Quality Delta:** A modest improvement in qualified mechanism density is plausible because the additions target hidden work, incentive mismatch, missing complements, and boundary conflict. It is not guaranteed. If the additions make outputs longer or cause more discovery turns without adding qualified distinct mechanisms, the per-1,000-token endpoint may worsen. The proper test is a paired, format-matched control with a preregistered minimum practical effect and variance-aware repetition count, not a generic brainstorming prompt or an exploratory pilot. A 100-case paired design can detect a moderate effect only after pilot variance, repetition count, and the minimum practically important improvement are frozen; public development data alone must not be presented as a significant primary-effect result.
- **Pass 5 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **REVISE.** Before implementation, freeze definitions for Process Tweak Depletion Rate, Failure-Mode Span, and Invariant Preservation. Report Vendi Score as a judge-free diagnostic, not proof of causal novelty. Use a format-matched control, paired case-level analysis, and preregistered quality gates. Treat any expected gain as a hypothesis, not as a forecast.

## Final Self-Reflective Synthesis & Strategic Recommendation

The v2 direction is worth pursuing, but the repository should **revise the plan before touching canonical files**. The current v1 architecture is already strong enough that the new additions should be **surgical contracts**, not a new theoretical layer. The right change is to keep the public method lightweight while making a few internal requirements explicit and testable:

1. In the root, add a compact structural-scan rule that names the four failure modes and the three boundary probes in operational language, not academic terms.
2. In `references/innovation-lenses.md`, add a short **Systemic Failure Sweep** that requires the model to check incentive mismatch, missing complement, network pathology, and legitimation buffer before finalizing the portfolio.
3. In `references/deep-mode.md`, use plain, one-at-a-time discovery questions and choose only the probe that fits the current tension.
4. In `SKILL.md`’s preservation logic, add a strict precedence rule: statutory, safety, privacy, rights, medical, contractual, and physical constraints are **invariants**; only replaceable procedures, tools, and habits are **vehicles**.
5. In the benchmark plan, freeze exact definitions for Process Tweak Depletion Rate, Failure-Mode Span, and Invariant Preservation before implementation. Treat Vendi Score as a diagnostic and require a paired, format-matched control plus human-calibrated quality gates for any headline claim.
6. Do **not** add an eighth Reasoning Cell and do **not** introduce CESM or MLP notation into the public skill. Their useful signal can be represented as internal dimensions of actors, environment, structure, mechanism, scale, and transition buffer.

If those revisions are made, the v2 evolution is likely to improve real-world usefulness and causal diversity while preserving portability, safety, and the ideation-first boundary. If they are skipped, the plan risks becoming either invisible to smaller models or a soft bypass that weakens preservation. The architecture should therefore proceed only after these specific adjustments are added to the v2 design contract and verified with realistic tests.
