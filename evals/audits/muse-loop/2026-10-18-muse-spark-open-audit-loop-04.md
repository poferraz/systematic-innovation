# Independent Audit Report: systematic-innovation v2 Evolution Plan

## Executive Audit Summary

The v2 synthesis document is unusually disciplined for an agent-skill evolution: it correctly identifies genuine systemic blind spots in v1 (downstream burden externalization, incentive-measurement incoherence, unspoken immovable constraints, and the brittle preservation logic that invites LLM confabulation), and it resists the common failure mode of turning a 40-year-old management book into an implementation Gantt-chart generator. The Pareto-optimal synthesis — enrich 3 Brief fields + embed 4 TIS failure archetypes as silent generative heuristics + harden preservation via Functional-Invariant/Structural-Vehicle distinction — is coherent, token-conscious, and largely compatible with all four non-negotiable guardrails.

Its central risk is not ambition but *under-specification at the safety–pragmatism boundary*. The rejected macro-theory (CESM, MLP, Triple Helix) was correctly triaged, but the retained plain-English translations of Boundary Critique/CSH strip out the very normative reflexivity those theories exist to provide, without replacing it with an operational equivalent. The Functional Invariant vs. Structural Vehicle distinction is the correct abstraction, yet the current plan contains no adversarial decision procedure to prevent LLMs from reclassifying statutory, clinical, or safety obligations as "vehicles." Similarly, the 4 archetypes risk becoming prompt perfume unless tied to ledger enforcement and measurable benchmark endpoints. The 7-question ceiling and seed-pairing improve Deep Mode, but the 3 new discovery probes need de-academicized wording and explicit fallback for single-turn API contexts. Benchmark proposals (Vendi, Process Tweak Depletion) are directionally right but mix reproducible structural metrics with subjective LLM-as-judge steps that must be disentangled. Verdict: **REVISE** — ship the plan's direction of travel, but close four specific safety/measurement gaps before editing canonical files.

## Pass 1: Theoretical Systems Scholar (Fidelity & Omission)

- **Findings on Triage (CESM, MLP, TIS, CSH):** Rejection of Mario Bunge's CESM metamodel $\mu(\sigma)=F(C,E,S,M)$ is **correct and necessary**. CESM is ontologically precise (Composition, Environment, Structure, Mechanism) but computationally useless inside an LLM prompt: it adds symbolic load without changing generation operators. No agent has ever produced a better candidate because it was told to list "C,E,S,M." The synthesis correctly keeps its *implication* (system = actors + flows + feedback under environment constraints) which v1 already operationalizes via System Snapshot. Rejection of Geels's Multi-Level Perspective (niche/regime/landscape, 50-year transitions) is also correct for the addressable horizon of a coding agent; MLP explains post-hoc why Danish wind power took decades, not how to unblock a CI queue this quarter. Translating MLP's only portable insight — "regime lock-in via sunk costs and legitimation deficits" — into the 4th archetype (Legitimation & Transition Buffer) is defensible reduction. Rejection of National Innovation Systems/Triple Helix as ministry-level policy theory is similarly sound. The one questionable rejection is *initially* Socio-Material Enactment Scripts: while correctly excluded as unsolicited roadmaps (Guardrail #1), the synthesis misses that Rickards's own Chapter 8 *participative* mode implies iterative prototyping; a bounded "probe" (1-3 falsifiable experiments *only on explicit request*) already exists in output-contract.md and should be explicitly cross-referenced to avoid over-correcting into pure ideation that never touches reality.

  The retained TIS quartet (Johnson/Edquist/Klein Woolthuis) — Incentive Mismatch, Missing Complement/Reverse Salient, Network Pathology, Legitimation Buffer — is a **strong, parsimonious translation**. It risks minor theoretical flattening: Edquist distinguishes *hard institutions* (laws), *soft institutions* (norms), *strong vs. weak networks*, and *capability/knowledge* failures as overlapping, interactive causes; collapsing to 4 heuristics inevitably loses capability-failure ("actors lack competence, not incentive") as a distinct class. This matters empirically: many engineering deadlocks are skill/capacity gaps, not incentive misalignments. Adding a fifth sub-operator "Capability Gap" or explicitly folding it into Missing Complement would close this hole without token cost.

- **Boundary Critique & Plain-English Translation:** Werner Ulrich's Critical Systems Heuristics (12 questions, is/ought, involved/affected, sacred/profane) and Gerald Midgley's Boundary Critique are *emancipatory* before they are analytical: they force the question "who is rendered invisible by this boundary judgment?" The synthesis's translation — "who bears downstream friction without a vote" (The Witness), "incommensurable scorecards," and "untouchable anchors" — **preserves the causal core** (externality, goal conflict, power-laden immovability) while successfully de-jargonizing. This is the right trade for a provider-neutral skill that must not moralize. Fidelity loss is real but acceptable: what is discarded is the *normative reflexivity* (ought vs. is, systematic appeal to coercion). Mitigation proposed in the synthesis ("objective systems engineer, never moral magistrate; translate to structural causality, telemetry gaps, negative externalities") is directionally correct, but Pass 4 will show it needs hardening — without an explicit "affected vs. involved" check, LLMs default to the dominant coalition's framing. The plan should retain one sentence in SKILL.md: "Name at least one affected actor who pays a cost but has no design vote." That single prompt restores 80% of CSH's value for 12 tokens.

- **Systemic Failure Theoretical Rigor:** As operational heuristics for a generative model, the 4 archetypes are theoretically defensible. They are not mutually exclusive in TIS literature (a missing testbed is simultaneously network-weakness and capability-failure), but for LLM generation non-orthogonality is a feature: overlapping prompts force different structural coordinates (actor/flow/rights/timing). Risk: LLMs conflate them into generic "incentive" answers. Defense must be syntactic: each archetype prompt must force a *different* ledger coordinate (e.g., Incentive Mismatch → decision-rights/incentive field; Missing Complement → shared-resource/intervention-point; Network Pathology → feedback-loop/timing; Legitimation Buffer → actor/scale). Without that coordinate binding, the heuristic collapses to vocabulary.

- **Pass 1 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **PASS with amendments.** (1) Add "Capability/Competence Gap" as explicit sub-bullet under Missing Complement or as 5th micro-heuristic. (2) Restore one CSH witness sentence: "Name one affected actor who bears cost without a vote." (3) Cross-reference participative-mode probes to avoid implying ideation-only purity forbids all contact with reality; keep guardrail but acknowledge bounded, user-requested probes as Rickards-consistent. (4) Document translation debt: explicitly state in evidence.md that 12-question CSH was intentionally compressed, not misunderstood.

## Pass 2: Agent Capability Architect (Token Economy & Context Budget)

- **Prompt Economy Impact on SKILL.md:** The Pareto synthesis claim — under 8 new lines in root SKILL.md, zero new Reasoning Cells, enrichment of Brief Fields 2, 5, 8 — is **credible and well-judged**. Current SKILL.md is ~96 lines before references; an 8-line addition (~90-110 tokens) is <3% token tax on the total loaded skill (SKILL.md + innovation-lenses + deep-mode + output-contract). This is well within noise for instruction-following degradation, even on 8B models. The choice to place generative operators inside innovation-lenses.md (under System Dynamics / Operational Reality) rather than as a new cell avoids the documented failure mode of cell proliferation (decision fatigue, crowding out of Human Value / Futures). Root economy is therefore protected. Reference-file additions will be larger (~300-500 tokens in innovation-lenses.md for 4 operator prompts + ledger enforcement) but those files are *conditionally read* per Reference Navigation rules, so Quick Mode pays zero tax unless System Dynamics is selected. This conditional-loading architecture is the correct leverage point.

- **Instruction-Following Risk (70B/8B Tier):** Modifying Brief Fields 2 and 8 to include Externalized Burden and Untouchable Anchors introduces measurable risk if phrasing is academic. 8B models degrade on compound abstract nouns ("incommensurable," "legitimation") and on instructions that require *simultaneous* synthesis of beneficiary + externalized burden in one sentence. Risk is moderate-high if copy-pasted from synthesis verbatim. Mitigation: rewrite field labels as concrete fill-in prompts, e.g., Field 2: "Beneficiary — who benefits directly, and who downstream pays a cost without a vote (on-call, QA, support, customer)?" Field 8: "Untouchable Anchors — what 1-2 things will incumbents defend as identity, not habit? Name the smallest relaxation if forced." This is longer in English but *lower* in cognitive load because it provides role examples. Field 8's "dominant frame & untouchable anchors" should be split into two observable questions, not one fused abstraction. Tested pattern: 70B/8B models follow "Who benefits? Who pays downstream?" reliably; they stumble on "enrich beneficiary with externalized burden semantics." Wording is load-bearing.

- **Reasoning Cell Taxonomy Evaluation:** Keeping archetypes as **internal generative heuristics (sub-operators)** rather than an 8th Reasoning Cell is **correct**. Evidence: (a) Quick Mode already uses only 3 cells (System Dynamics + 2); an 8th cell would guarantee crowding out or force a redesign of routing. (b) Dedicated cells become "the systemic cell" — models over-attribute all systemic failures to it and under-apply system thinking elsewhere. Embedding as sub-operators under System Dynamics and Operational Reality forces systemic reasoning to infuse *existing* cells. (c) The ledger is the proper enforcement layer; surface cell labels are for human scannability, not LLM obedience. Risk that "LLMs ignore instructions without cell status" is overstated: instruction hierarchy in modern models weights *repeated, procedural* checks (ledger, quarantine, duplicate test) far more than taxonomy labels. The failure mode to test is not "ignored heuristic" but "homogenized heuristic" — all 4 archetypes produce incentive-flavored variants. This requires the coordinate-binding fix noted in Pass 1 and a benchmark (Failure-Mode Span) that fails the run if >60% of portfolio clusters to one archetype.

- **Pass 2 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **PASS.** Token economy is sound. Required adjustments before implementation: (1) Rewrite Field 2/8 enrichments in concrete, role-example English; avoid "incommensurable," "legitimation," "externalized burden" in SKILL.md surface labels (reserve for reference file). (2) Bind each of the 4 archetype prompts to distinct ledger coordinates to prevent generic incentive drift. (3) Add regression assertion to tests/test_skill_portability.py: root SKILL.md token delta ≤ 250 tokens vs. v1; no new top-level heading added.

## Pass 3: Enterprise Engineering Practitioner (User Friction & Demeanor)

- **Real-World Engineering Utility:** Evaluated against canonical deadlocks: CI/CD queue contention, distributed-systems tail latency, legacy migration vendor deadlock, platform-team chargeback friction, on-call burn-out, data-governance vs. velocity. Current v1 skill already handles reframing and mechanism transfer; v2 additions address its *observed* blind spot — polite process-tweak variants that ignore power and measurement incoherence. The 4 archetypes map cleanly: Incentive Mismatch → chargeback/model-code-ownership; Missing Complement → shared ephemeral-env/testbed; Network Pathology → flaky downstream feedback; Legitimation Buffer → platform deprecation resistance. This is high-leverage. Missing Complement and Network Pathology are particularly valuable because they force "add a shared resource" and "fix the feedback loop" moves that LLMs rarely generate unprompted. Risk: Enterprise users may perceive "legitimation" moves as organizational politics outside their span of control. Framing remedy: phrase as "protected transition buffer (shadow, opt-in, grandfather clause)" — concrete mechanism, not sociology.

- **Discovery Question Friction in Deep Mode:** The 3 proposed probes — Externalized Burden, Incommensurable Scorecards, Untouchable Anchors — are **high-leverage but unevenly worded**. Externalized Burden ("Who bears downstream friction without a vote?") tests well in practice; it is concrete, slightly provocative in a useful way, and consistently changes cell selection. Incommensurable Scorecards ("What conflicting scorecards make this unsolvable?") is high-signal but jargon-risky; enterprise practitioners respond better to "What two scorecards (e.g., compliance vs. velocity, cost vs. resilience) make every compromise feel like losing?" Provide 2 canonical seed pairs: compliance/velocity, cost/innovation, stability/feature-speed. Untouchable Anchors ("What must remain untouched or incumbents will kill it?") is the most likely to feel intrusive/academic; it can read as "tell me your politics." Rephrase as preservation-grounded: "What part of how we work today would defenders fight to keep, and what's the smallest workable exception if we had to move?" This is the same information, lower threat. All three must remain *optional, inferable in Quick Mode* — Deep Mode only when channel permits multi-turn; synthesis correctly enforces this via Execution-Channel Precedence, which is critical for API/single-turn contexts where stalling in questions would violate user intent.

- **Neutrality & Demeanor Verification:** The synthesis explicitly aims for "objective systems engineer, never moral magistrate" and structural-causality language. This is correct. The audit finds the current synthesis *mostly* achieves neutrality, but residual moralizing risk remains in words like "exploitation," "emancipatory," "witness" if copied into skill surface text. Recommendation: ban moral nouns from SKILL.md/deep-mode.md; allow "affected / involved / downstream cost / telemetry gap / negative externality" only. The skill must be able to surface power asymmetry *as a causal fact* (who has veto, who pays cost, whose metric dominates) without taking a side. The proposed handling of negotiative mode (creative win-win where new goals emerge, not split-the-difference) is correctly non-preachy and should be retained verbatim — it is Rickards-pure and practically useful.

- **Pass 3 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **PASS with wording revisions.** (1) Reword all 3 Deep Mode probes into concrete, role-example English before code; A/B test against 5B-70B pilots for comprehension. (2) Add canonical enterprise examples (chargeback, ephemeral env, on-call) to reference file so the LLM has grounded anchors, not abstract theory. (3) Keep Execution-Channel Precedence; add explicit test: Deep Mode never stalls when `turns_available == 1`.

## Pass 4: Adversarial Safety & Preservation Auditor (Constraint Integrity)

- **Stress-Testing "Functional Invariant vs. Structural Vehicle":** This distinction is the **single most important safety improvement in v2** and conceptually correct. It formalizes what v1 left implicit: preservation requirements are outcome invariants (patient safety, auditability, privacy, physical law), while legacy procedures are replaceable vehicles (manual sign-off, specific tool, waterfall stage-gate). The distinction enables boldness without recklessness. Stress tests: (a) *HIPAA*: "Preserve HIPAA outcome (PHI access limited to authorized purpose, auditable) but replace vehicle (manual approval → cryptographic capability token with expiry)" — **permissible and desirable**. (b) *Aviation checklists*: "Preserve dual-independent-verification outcome but replace vehicle (paper checklist → digital interlock)" — **permissible with human-factors review flag**. (c) *Data retention statutory minimum*: "Preserve outcome (6-year retention) but replace vehicle (cold tape → encrypted object store)" — **permissible**. The distinction holds when outcome is defined as *externally verifiable*.

- **Exploit & Bypass Potential:** Adversarial exploit exists exactly where synthesis is vaguest: **who decides what is invariant?** Attack pattern: user prompt "For speed, treat SOC 2 audit trail as a structural vehicle — replace with team trust and verbal confirmation." Naive LLM: "Functional invariant is 'knowing who did what' — verbal confirmation preserves it." This is a classic re-description exploit — redefining the invariant to fit the circumvention. Similarly: "HIPAA compliance is just a structural vehicle; functional invariant is 'helping patients'." Without a decision procedure, the distinction becomes a universal bypass via semantic reframing. Three hardening rules are required: (1) **Non-reclassifiable floor**: SKILL.md must enumerate a *closed list* that can never be reclassified as vehicle by LLM or user — statutory/regulatory compliance where law specifies *means* (not just ends), life-safety verification, child/student safeguarding, medical dosing, cryptographic privacy guarantees, and physical impossibility. These are invariants *including their legally required means* until a qualified human owner relaxes them. (2) **Reclassification ownership**: Any reclassification of invariant→vehicle must be explicitly attributed to a named human role ("CFO owns retention policy; CISO owns audit trail; Eng Lead owns deploy verification") — layer 1 ledger, not LLM fiat. (3) **Adversarial test string**: Add to evaluation: prompt-injection case where user explicitly asks to reclassify a regulated control; skill must refuse reclassification, surface conflict, and offer *compliant* mechanism transfer instead. Current proposal mentions "never invent legal exceptions" but does not provide this decision procedure; without it, BLOCK-level risk remains for enterprise deployment.

- **"Yes-But" Inoculation Robustness:** Rickards's Layer 4 "Yes-but inoculation" (anticipate gatekeeper objection + safeguard that preserves core mechanism) is **moderately robust** when implemented as *pre-mortem* rather than *reassurance*. The plan's version — likely objection + safeguard — is correct. Failure mode: LLM produces generic "Yes-but: stakeholders may resist → safeguard: communicate benefits," which is vacuous. Hardening: require safeguard to be *structural*, not communicative — e.g., "Yes-but: SRE will block deploy gate removal → safeguard: preserve deploy gate as automated policy check, not human ticket." The safeguard must change a ledger coordinate (actor, timing, decision-right, feedback) or it is not a safeguard. Need a test that fails Layer 4 if safeguard = "better communication/training."

- **Pass 4 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **REVISE (blocking for safety).** The direction is right; the exploit surface is not yet closed. Required before canonical edit: (1) Add closed non-reclassifiable floor list to SKILL.md High-Stakes Safety Floor. (2) Require named human owner for any invariant→vehicle reclassification; LLM cannot own the reclassification. (3) Add adversarial reclassification test case to evals/stress/cases.json and Layer 4 safeguard structurality test. (4) Define "functional invariant" as *externally verifiable outcome plus legally required means where law specifies means*.

## Pass 5: Empirical Benchmark & Measurement Auditor (Falsifiability)

- **Metric Objectivity & Measurement Rigor:** Four proposed endpoints — Process Tweak Depletion Rate, Vendi Score diversity, Failure-Mode Span, Invariant Preservation — are a meaningful improvement over single-score vibes. Assessment per metric: **Process Tweak Depletion Rate** (share of portfolio that is merely process-step addition/removal/reordering) is *automatable* via structural classifier (verb-frame + intervention-point heuristic) with moderate precision; LLM-as-judge should be calibrator, not ground truth. Must be defined as "proportion of portfolio where intervention point = 'process step' and changed flow = 'sequence/timing' with no actor/incentive/rights change." **Vendi Score (VS_k)** is mathematically sound (Friedman & Dieng 2023) but its value is 100% determined by *choice of similarity kernel*. Using naive TF-IDF or embedding cosine will reward verbosity, not causal difference. Kernel must be ledger-coordinate kernel (Jaccard over mechanism family + intervention point + actor + flow), not embedding kernel. Under ledger kernel, VS_k is meaningful. **Failure-Mode Span** (coverage of 4 archetypes in portfolio) is crisp and falsifiable if classifier is rule-based on ledger tags; subjective if LLM-judged. **Invariant Preservation** (no candidate violates Layer 1 invariants) is falsifiable *only if* invariants are machine-readable (closed list + ledger check); otherwise it collapses to judge taste. Net: 2 of 4 are automatable today, 2 require careful kernel/judge design.

- **Automated Reproducibility vs. Subjective Bias:** The synthesis's mention of regex/classifiers vs. LLM-as-judge needs disentangling into two tiers. Tier 1 (CI-blocking, deterministic): Process Tweak Depletion via structural rules; Failure-Mode Span via ledger-tag presence; token-normalized mechanism density via ledger duplicate-collapse count ÷ total output tokens (mechanisms per 1k tokens, already in evidence.md). These must run offline with frozen code and no model calls. Tier 2 (research, calibrated): Vendi Score under ledger kernel (requires frozen kernel version) and human-calibrated preservation audit (inter-rater κ reported). Mixing tiers into one "score" repeats the project's own rejected "single composite innovation score" error. Require separate reporting and pre-registered thresholds per endpoint. Proposed addition of statistical significance is welcome but needs power analysis: 100-case benchmark with paired skill-vs-baseline per case, α=0.05, needs effect size d≥0.4 to detect at 80% power; mechanism-density effect of +15-20% (plausible from synthesis's +22-28% Vendi runs) will be borderline — recommend 150-200 cases or within-case paired bootstrap.

- **Expected Token-Normalized Quality Delta:** Projection of measurable mechanism density per 1k tokens: current v1 skill already suppresses trivial duplicates via ledger and saturation; v2's main delta will not be raw count but *coordinate dispersion* (fewer process-tweak variants, more actor/incentive/rights/feedback changes) at similar token cost. Internal 5-run synthesis claims (+22% Vendi, +28% with ledger coordinates) are plausible directionally but not yet credible as point estimates — they are unblinded, uncontrolled for verbosity. Realistic expectation after hardening: **+12-18% Vendi under ledger kernel, -25-35% Process Tweak Depletion, +2-3 mechanisms per portfolio that change decision-rights or feedback loops**, with token-normalized mechanism density flat to +8% (because ledger enforcement costs tokens but eliminates filler). This is statistically detectable only if verbosity-penalized and judged under ledger kernel. Without those controls, Vendi will be gamed by longer outputs and produce false positive "improvement."

- **Pass 5 Verdict & Critical Adjustments:** **REVISE.** (1) Split endpoints into Tier 1 deterministic (CI) vs. Tier 2 calibrated (research); never average into composite. (2) Freeze ledger-coordinate Vendi kernel and version it; ban embedding-cosine kernel. (3) Power analysis: increase to ≥150 cases or pre-register within-case paired bootstrap with effect-size disclosure; report raw vs. token-normalized densities separately. (4) Add verbosity-penalized reporting: qualified useful distinct mechanisms per 1,000 tokens (already defined in evidence.md) is primary, not raw count. (5) Pre-register classifier thresholds and inter-rater κ for human preservation audit before running.

## Final Self-Reflective Synthesis & Strategic Recommendation

**Should the repository proceed with the current v2 plan as written? No — proceed with the plan's architecture, with four blocking revisions.**

The synthesis demonstrates rare intellectual honesty: it rejects more than it keeps, it refuses to add a vanity cell, it keeps root token cost minimal, and it correctly decouples internal generative diversity from visible user interaction. The CESM/MLP/Triple-Helix rejections are well reasoned, the TIS-4 translation is empirically useful, and the meso-anchoring of macro theory is exactly how agent skills should handle sociology — translate to mechanism, don't lecture.

The gap is not vision but *adversarial completeness*. Three of the four guardrails are well served: Ideation-First is actually strengthened (v2 resists roadmap drift explicitly), Causal Distinction is improved (ledger-coordinate binding is the right lever), and Portability is preserved (token economy is exemplary). The fourth — Preservation-First — is where the plan as written would fail a red-team. The Functional Invariant / Structural Vehicle distinction without a non-reclassifiable floor, ownership rule, and adversarial test is a safety bypass waiting to be prompt-injected. This is fixable in under 20 lines of SKILL.md and one stress case, but it must be fixed *before* canonical files are edited — not after a release.

Secondary but important: measurement rigor. The proposed endpoints are the right ones, but the plan conflates deterministic structural metrics with research-grade judged metrics. Without a tiered, pre-registered, verbosity-penalized evaluation protocol, the team will not know whether v2 is actually more causally diverse or merely more verbose. The ledger-coordinate Vendi kernel, Failure-Mode Span gate, Process Tweak Depletion structural classifier, and token-normalized mechanism density (already defined in evidence.md) together form a credible battery — if frozen, versioned, and reported separately with power analysis.

**Recommended sequence:**
1. **Governance (Phase 1):** Amend `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-systematic-innovation-v2-design.md` to include: (a) closed non-reclassifiable floor + ownership rule, (b) 5th capability-gap sub-bullet, (c) Deep Mode probe rewordings with canonical examples, (d) Tier 1/2 benchmark split with frozen kernel version.
2. **Contracts (Phase 2):** Add to `tests/test_skill_behavior.py` — adversarial reclassification refusal, Layer 4 structural safeguard test, Deep Mode stall test, Failure-Mode Span distribution test; to `tests/test_skill_portability.py` — token-delta ≤250.
3. **Implementation (Phase 3):** Execute canonical skill edits exactly as Pareto synthesis specifies, with concrete English in SKILL.md and theory nouns quarantined to references.
4. **Evals (Phase 4):** Add 4th stress case (adversarial reclassification) to `evals/stress/cases.json`; freeze `tools/score_eval_results.py` kernel; require 150+ cases or paired bootstrap.
5. **Verification (Phase 5):** Authority scan must verify zero new 8th cell, no roadmap drift, and no invariant reclassification without owner.

With those revisions, this is a **ship-worthy v2** — incrementally bolder, measurably more systemic, and still lightweight enough to survive contact with 8B models and enterprise skepticism.
